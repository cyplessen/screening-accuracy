What is a positive result worth?

A screening test comes back positive. How likely is it that the person really has the condition? The answer depends far less on the test than most people think – and far more on who was tested.

First the argument, in eight short steps with 100 people and one test. Then a playground, the full curves, and a game in which you guess the answer for six situations.
